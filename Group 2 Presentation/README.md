# EXPLORING GOOGLE FORMS & GOOGLE MEET

Explores the core features, purposes, and collaborative capabilities of Google Forms and Google Meet within the Google Workspace ecosystem. Covers digital data collection, automated quizzes, form customization, virtual conferencing, screen sharing, breakout rooms, live captions, and seamless workspace integration for modern online communication.

### Presentation Overview

#### 📝 Part 1: Google Forms
* **Core Purpose:** Electronic data collection, surveys, questionnaires, feedback gathering, and self-grading quizzes.
* **Features & Question Types:** Supports multiple-choice, checkboxes, dropdowns, linear scales, ratings, file uploads, grids, date/time fields, section branching, and Google Sheets integration.

#### 🎥 Part 2: Google Meet
* **Core Purpose:** Real-time virtual meetings, live screen sharing, and direct scheduling via Gmail and Google Calendar[cite: 59, 60, 61].
* **Collaboration & Accessibility:** Includes AI noise cancellation, visual effects, interactive whiteboarding, breakout rooms, live polling, Q&A, real-time captions, and host moderation controls.
