# MAKING A WEBSITE

Showcases the design and development of an educational advocacy website titled **"Aging with Dignity: Mental Health Awareness for Senior Citizens in the Philippines."** Demonstrates AI-assisted web prototyping, accessible UI/UX design, responsive layouts, and culturally sensitive digital storytelling to promote intergenerational understanding and reduce mental health stigma.
